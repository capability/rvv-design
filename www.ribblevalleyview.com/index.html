Good to know

Questions, gently answered.

What does a 12-month holiday licence mean?

Owners can enjoy their lodge 365 days a year — though it remains a holiday home, not a primary residence. Combined with our 50-year site licence, ownership offers genuine flexibility.

Are dogs welcome?

Always. Most of our lodges are dog-friendly for both stays and ownership — and the footpaths around the farm were practically designed for them.

How short can a break be?

Three-night weekends (Fri–Mon) and four-night midweek (Mon–Fri) — or stay the full week, Fri–Fri. Short breaks from £150, weekly from £260.

Can we view the show lodges?

Yes — by private appointment. We’ll show you around the park, the plots and the show lodges. Get in touch and we’ll book you in.
